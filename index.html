II. Blanc de Blancs — The Read

I went through Delamotte's actual site before writing this.

It's a simple structure, but a deliberate one: a yes/no age check before anything else loads, four cuvées that each get their own full page instead of sitting together in one crowded grid, and gift sets treated as their own quiet category.

Age check first

Gates the whole site before anything loads — not a banner you click past.

Four full pages

Each cuvée gets its own page, not a tile in a crowded collection grid.

Gift sets, set apart

Their own quiet category — not bundled in with the everyday range.

None of that's technically hard to build in Shopify. But it has to live inside the theme itself, not get bolted on as a popup app, or it'll feel cheap next to everything else on the page.
